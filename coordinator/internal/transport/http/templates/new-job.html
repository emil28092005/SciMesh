What you will observe

  1. Input accepted
    Dataset is validated and split into durable shard artifacts.
  2. Workers claim tasks
    Each worker downloads one shard, computes, and uploads a partial result.
  3. Global reduction
    The coordinator reduces all partials into one final artifact.
  4. Final download
    The result page exposes a checksum-protected result file.
